# New Creature Concepts

This document contains ideas for new creatures to add to the Fantasy Combat Card Game, organized by element. These additions are designed to balance the elemental distribution and introduce new tactical options using the existing ability pool.

## Water Creatures
*Water currently only has one creature (Water Elemental). These new additions will help flesh out this element.*

### 1. Siren
* **Element:** Water
* **Description:** A mesmerizing creature that uses magic and agility to control the battlefield.
* **Stats:**
  * Strength: 30
  * Agility: 75
  * Intelligence: 85
  * Defense: 40
  * Magic: 90
* **Abilities:** `EVASION`, `CAST_SPELL`
* **Health:** 280
* **Immunities:** None
* **Role:** High-evasion magic attacker.

### 2. Leviathan
* **Element:** Water
* **Description:** A massive sea beast with immense strength and defensive capabilities.
* **Stats:**
  * Strength: 95
  * Agility: 30
  * Intelligence: 40
  * Defense: 90
  * Magic: 50
* **Abilities:** `SHIELD_WALL`, `WATER_BLAST`
* **Health:** 500
* **Immunities:** `burning`
* **Role:** A tanky physical brawler similar to the Dragon or Earth Elemental.
